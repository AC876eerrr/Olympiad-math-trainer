FAQ

Quick answers before you dive in.

Which contests does the problem bank cover?

AMC 8, AMC 10, AMC 12, AIME, and USAJMO. Problems are tagged by contest, topic, and difficulty so you can drill exactly what you need.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan includes 500 problems, difficulty up to 14, 10 strategy methods, and RPG Chapter 1 — forever, no credit card required.

What does Pro add?

Everything: all 12,000 problems, elite difficulty 11–20, all 50 strategy methods, all 6 RPG chapters, and advanced analytics. $8/month or $60/year.

Do I need an account?

Yes — an account saves your progress, XP, Math Cash, bookmarks, notes, and RPG state, and it works across devices.

How hard is "elite" difficulty?

The 11–20 scale runs from late-AMC 12 / early AIME territory all the way to problems harder than the typical USAJMO 1/4. Level 20 is designed to be brutally hard — most solvers never clear it.

What is Math Cash?

Math Cash (M$) is the in-app reward currency you earn for solving problems, winning RPG battles, and hitting streaks. Spend it on RPG upgrades and perks.
